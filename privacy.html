Privacy

Updated October 7, 2026. This notice describes the website, optional accounts and payments.

Playing & browser storage

You can play without an account. Host game progress, player names, character assignments, votes, guest reconnection tokens and locally saved mysteries are stored in your browser. They remain until removed by the app or you clear the relevant browser data. Use a trusted device; anyone with access to its saved host game can inspect the story and solution.

The host sends game state to connected guests over PeerJS/WebRTC. Guests receive their own character packet and the public evidence and votes. PeerJS signaling helps browsers find each other; network providers and peers may receive connection metadata, including IP addresses. This is not an anonymous communication service. Do not put sensitive personal information into player names or stories.

Local game progress is not uploaded as an account gameplay history. Ending a game clears the active host save but does not delete saved mystery copies or other devices' browser data.

Optional accounts & legacy story records

Supabase provides email/password authentication and the community database. It processes account email, authentication credentials and sessions; signup and recovery emails use Resend. Login tokens are saved in this browser to maintain and refresh your session. Signing out removes this browser's current login session. Passwords are handled by the authentication service, not included in game packets.

Story creation, submissions and community publishing are no longer offered. Previously stored drafts, revision history, submissions and moderation records are not automatically deleted by this change. Previously saved device drafts and story copies may also remain in browser storage, but are no longer accessible through the site editor. Contact the owner about legacy records or deletion.

Signing out does not delete your account or cloud records. Local story deletion is not a request to delete an account or moderation history. For access, correction or deletion questions, use the contact route to ask for a private conversation without posting account information publicly. There is no self-service account-deletion screen yet.

Optional payments & purchased games

PayPal hosts payment and card checkout. We do not receive or store full card numbers or security codes. PayPal processes your payment information under its own privacy policy, including any information you provide for guest-card checkout.

Our protected community database links your game account to the bundle purchase, amount, currency, PayPal order/capture references, payment status, purchase-terms consent and payment-event history. Server-verified completed payments determine access. Administrators can view payment records and account email for support and refunds. Refunds and reversals remove access from the affected purchase. These records are retained for account access, reconciliation, disputes and applicable recordkeeping; signing out is not deletion.

Premium phone rooms store the host account reference, player names, roles, actions, ballots and match state in a protected server database. Guests receive only their own private role and report, plus public game information; the host's room controls do not reveal other players' private cards or choices. Each phone stores a secret reconnect token locally; the server stores only its hash. Anyone with access to that phone's browser data can access its seat. Keep room codes within your group and do not share tokens. Rooms expire after 24 hours; expired records are deleted when a new premium room is created. Closing a room stops access immediately but does not immediately delete its record. Payment refunds or disputes suspend hosted-room access.

Optional pass-and-play match snapshots save in this browser under an account-specific key, not in cloud purchase history. They contain all roles and match state: use a trusted device. Signing out hides the playable session but leaves this browser's match save so it can resume after signing in. Discard saved pass-and-play match removes that local snapshot without removing ownership.

Payment questions and refund requests use the private support email shown in the shop before live purchases open. Do not post receipts, account details or payment information in the public GitHub tracker.

External services

The site no longer offers AI story generation or review and makes no story-assistant provider requests. AI keys and settings saved by an older version may remain in that browser until its site data is cleared. Clearing site data also removes local game progress and login sessions.

Cloudflare and GitHub Pages host the website. Google Fonts supplies fonts, and external links take you to services with their own policies. Those services receive ordinary network request information.

Traffic monitoring

Grim Gatherings uses Cloudflare Web Analytics on its production domain to understand visits, page views, referring sites, device/browser categories and page performance. Reports are private to the website owner; there is no public visitor counter.

Cloudflare describes Web Analytics as cookie-free and says it does not track individual visitors. Visits and page views are not a count of unique people. Blocking, repeat visits and bot detection affect totals.

We do not add account emails, story contents, room codes, passwords or login tokens to custom analytics events. We do not use analytics to measure individual player behavior or story completion.
