Traffic monitoring

Grim Gatherings uses Cloudflare Web Analytics on its production domain to understand visits, page views, referring sites, device/browser categories, and page performance. Reports are available privately to the website owner; there is no public visitor counter.

Cloudflare describes Web Analytics as cookie-free and says it does not track individual visitors. Visits and page views are not a count of unique people. Browser blocking, repeat visits, and bot detection can affect the totals.

We do not add account emails, story contents, room codes, passwords, or login tokens to custom analytics events. This website does not use analytics to measure story completion or individual player behavior.

This notice describes traffic monitoring only. Accounts and story submissions use the separate Supabase community service; optional AI features send the requested prompt to your selected provider when you choose to use them.
