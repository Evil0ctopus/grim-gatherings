How to play

A mystery night with friends. One host narrates; everyone reads, discusses and votes.

Quick start

Choose Create a new game, add your players and pick a story that fits your group. Open the lobby and share its QR code. Follow the reader prompts until the final reveal.

Both the host and guest phones support the listed main-site mysteries: Woodland Hollow, Blackwater Row, Ravenmoor, The Lago Cabin, LOCKDOWN and the Briar House playtest. Additional testing stories remain available on the testing website. Choose the player count shown on each mystery's card.

Woodland Hollow uses exactly 14 players and six rounds. Dead characters remain players and read their ghost memories. Discuss after each round, then continue to the next chapter or the author's ending; this mystery does not use ballots.

Start a gathering

How it works

  1. Choose your mystery. Every story has one fixed cast size. Add that many players, assign characters and open the lobby.
  2. Meet the cast. Guests join on their phones without accounts. The host reads the setup aloud, then each player reads their character card. Discuss the introduction if you like.
  3. Read, discuss, vote. The turn banner tells you who reads next. Everyone reads one clue each round, then the group deliberates and votes. Make final accusations and a final vote before hearing the full story and its fixed reveal.

Devices

A laptop or tablet is ideal for the host; guests use their phones. Use a current Chrome or Safari browser and an internet connection on every device. Keep the host screen open and awake.

Mafia is a separate hidden-role game with fresh random roles each time. Choose everyone's-phone mode or one-phone narrator mode; its screen walks you through night, discussion and voting.

Host guide

Preparing & running a story

Review the character assignments before opening the lobby. The host counts as a player only if assigned a character. The review contains spoilers, so keep it private from players.

Read the narration aloud and call on the reader named in the turn banner. Mark each clue read. Every player reads once per round; discussion and voting follow every round.

Murderer identity notification is off by default. The host can enable it during character review. Ghost parts appear only where the story calls for them; they stay in the reading order.

Clue order is precomputed. Each player reads about every other character before any story-required repeats, which the story notes explain. You do not need to manage the coverage math.

Connections, rejoining & saved games

The host browser runs free-story and everyone's-phone Mafia rooms. A closed laptop, locked device or suspended browser cannot keep a room running, even when sleep prevention is available.

If someone disconnects, leave their character assigned. Phones retry automatically and keep their last received clues; voting needs a connection. Return to the host screen or choose Reconnect room, rather than creating a new room.

To reopen a saved game, use Resume on the same browser and device. Guests keep the same link and character. Browser Back preserves your game; creating a new game or replacing a mystery asks for confirmation.

Clearing browser data can lose progress. Accounts keep premium purchase access, not free-game cloud backups. Restrictive networks may block peer connections; try another network or browser.

Premium rooms

Only the host buys. In the shop, choose Host a room and share its eight-character code. Guests join free through Join a room, without accounts.

Each premium story has five players and uses the same read-aloud story loop. Check lobby names before starting. A playing host joins a player seat too; a non-playing host uses only host controls.

Reconnect in the same browser and finish within 24 hours. Pass-and-play is also available on one trusted device. Purchases may be paused at times; the shop shows availability and pricing.

Contact & support

Report bugs through our GitHub support tracker. Include your browser, device and steps to reproduce. A GitHub account is needed to post.

Reports are public. Never post passwords, tokens, live room links or personal information. For account or privacy questions, ask the owner for a private contact method.
